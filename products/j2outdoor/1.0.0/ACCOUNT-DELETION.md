# J2Outdoor Account Deletion

J2Outdoor is provided by Voltika Labs. This page explains how a J2Outdoor user
can request deletion of an account and its associated personal data.

J2Outdoor customers do not create or use an application account. Accounts are
used only by authorized administrators. A customer who wants to request access,
correction, or deletion of data associated with a rental request or completed
rental should use the same email address with the subject **J2Outdoor Customer
Data Request**. Transaction records subject to the retention rules below are
not treated as an active customer account.

## Submit a deletion request

Send an email to
[contact.j2outdoor@gmail.com](mailto:contact.j2outdoor@gmail.com?subject=J2Outdoor%20Account%20Deletion%20Request)
with the subject **J2Outdoor Account Deletion Request**.

Include the username or email address registered to the account. Do not send a
password, government identity document, payment credential, or other secret.
Voltika Labs may reply using the registered contact channel and request limited
information needed to verify account ownership before deletion.

## What will be deleted

After ownership is verified and the request is accepted, Voltika Labs will
permanently delete the account profile and personal data that is no longer
needed to provide J2Outdoor services within 30 days. Account credentials and
active session tokens will also be invalidated. Aggregated or permanently
de-identified information that can no longer be associated with the user may
remain.

Request drafts that do not become transactions are deleted 30 days after the
last activity. Minimum transaction, payment, deposit, return, and accounting
records may be retained for 10 years after the end of the relevant financial
year for legal, tax, accounting, audit, transaction-evidence, security, and
dispute-resolution purposes. Relevant records may be held longer while an
active transaction, suspected fraud, dispute, audit, or legal proceeding is in
progress. The response to the request will identify any data subject to such a
hold and the reason. Retained records are not used for marketing and are
deleted or permanently de-identified when the applicable period or hold ends.

Removing the J2Outdoor app from a device does not by itself delete an account.

## Bahasa Indonesia

Pelanggan J2Outdoor tidak membuat atau menggunakan akun aplikasi. Akun hanya
digunakan oleh admin yang berwenang. Pelanggan yang ingin meminta akses,
koreksi, atau penghapusan data terkait permintaan maupun transaksi rental dapat
menggunakan alamat email yang sama dengan subjek **Permintaan Data Pelanggan
J2Outdoor**. Catatan transaksi yang masih berada dalam masa retensi bukan akun
pelanggan aktif.

Untuk meminta penghapusan akun J2Outdoor dan data pribadi terkait, kirim email
ke
[contact.j2outdoor@gmail.com](mailto:contact.j2outdoor@gmail.com?subject=Permintaan%20Penghapusan%20Akun%20J2Outdoor)
dengan subjek **Permintaan Penghapusan Akun J2Outdoor**. Sertakan nama pengguna
atau alamat email yang terdaftar. Jangan mengirim kata sandi, dokumen identitas,
data pembayaran, atau rahasia lainnya.

Voltika Labs dapat melakukan verifikasi kepemilikan akun sebelum penghapusan.
Setelah permintaan terverifikasi, akun, kredensial, token sesi aktif, dan data
profil yang tidak lagi diperlukan akan dihapus paling lambat 30 hari. Draft
yang tidak menjadi transaksi dihapus 30 hari setelah aktivitas terakhir.
Catatan minimum mengenai transaksi, pembayaran, deposit, pengembalian, dan
pembukuan dapat disimpan selama 10 tahun sejak akhir tahun buku terkait untuk
kewajiban hukum, pajak, akuntansi, audit, pembuktian transaksi, keamanan, dan
penyelesaian sengketa. Data terkait dapat ditahan lebih lama selama transaksi,
dugaan penipuan, sengketa, audit, atau proses hukum masih berlangsung. Balasan
permintaan akan menjelaskan setiap penahanan dan alasannya. Catatan yang
dipertahankan tidak digunakan untuk pemasaran dan akan dihapus atau dianonimkan
secara permanen ketika masa retensi atau penahanan berakhir.

Menghapus aplikasi J2Outdoor dari perangkat tidak otomatis menghapus akun.
