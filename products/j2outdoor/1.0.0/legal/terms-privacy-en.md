# J2 OUTDOOR RENTAL TERMS & PRIVACY POLICY

**Version:** 2026-10-01-v11-draft

**Effective date:** 1 October 2026

This document explains the terms for requesting and carrying out an equipment rental and how J2 Outdoor processes Personal Data. Read the entire document before giving consent.

## 1. Scope and Consent

Before pressing **Send now**, you separately confirm that you have read and agree to the Rental Terms and that you have read the Privacy Policy. Reading the Privacy Policy does not constitute blanket consent to Personal Data processing. The submission records these declarations together with the document version, time, language, application version, and rental-request number.

Data necessary to handle your request and perform the rental is processed as described in sections 4 and 5. If the rental is confirmed, J2 Outdoor may require you to present your original KTP for identity verification and temporary physical collateral. The KTP is handled by an authorized J2 Outdoor administrator, not stored by the application. Before accepting it, the administrator must explain the custody period and return conditions and provide a handover record. The KTP is returned after the equipment and rental obligations are settled. If you cannot provide an original KTP, ask the administrator about an available alternative collateral arrangement.

If you do not agree, do not submit the request through the application. You may contact J2 Outdoor using the contact details in section 11 to ask about available service alternatives.

## 2. A Rental Request Is Not a Rental Confirmation

The WhatsApp message and application draft are an initial request, not confirmation of availability, final price, or a rental agreement. A rental takes effect only after an administrator checks stock, period, identity, collateral, price, deposit, and pickup terms and then confirms the transaction.

Prices shown in the application are estimates based on data available when the request is created. The final price, deposit, late fees, damage charges, and any other lawful charges must be explained and agreed before confirmation. J2 Outdoor must not impose charges that were not clearly disclosed.

## 3. Renter Responsibilities

The renter must provide accurate information, use equipment for its intended purpose and follow safety instructions, safeguard it during the rental, not transfer it without permission, and return it at the agreed time and in the agreed condition. Loss, damage, accidents, or delay must be reported promptly.

Condition inspections and responsibility for damage, delay, loss, cancellation, deposit return, and disputes will follow the transaction details confirmed by the administrator and Indonesian law. These terms do not remove consumer rights or exclude business liability where exclusion is prohibited by law.

If an original KTP is accepted as temporary physical collateral, it must be kept securely with access limited to authorized personnel, must not be used for an unrelated purpose, and must be returned according to the confirmed rental terms. The renter should keep the handover record and report any problem with the document immediately.

## 4. Personal Data We Process

At the request stage, we may process your name, WhatsApp number if provided, notes, selected equipment and sizes, quantities, rental period, estimated price, selected WhatsApp contact, request status, application version, and consent evidence.

Customers do not create or use J2Outdoor accounts. Customer data is stored only as a request draft or rental record under the flows and retention schedule in this document. Application accounts are used only by authorized administrators.

If the rental proceeds, we store name, telephone number, village, regency/city, transaction, payment and deposit information, equipment condition and return records, collateral receipt/return status, and relevant notes. In the administrator's customer-data workflow, OCR may process a selected or captured KTP image locally and temporarily to assist entry. The administrator must review and correct the extracted name, village, and regency/city before saving. The application does not retain the NIK, place/date of birth, full address, KTP image, or raw OCR output. Do not send an ID-card image in notes or through WhatsApp.

## 5. Purposes and Legal Bases

Data is used only as necessary to receive and respond to requests; check availability; create, perform, and complete rentals; verify identity and collateral; communicate; process payments, deposits, returns, damage, or disputes; protect equipment; comply with law; prevent misuse; and maintain system security and audit records.

Depending on the activity, the legal basis may be your explicit consent, steps taken at your request before entering a contract, performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, or a legitimate interest balanced against your rights, in accordance with applicable law.

## 6. Storage, Security, and Retention

Telephone numbers and draft content containing Personal Data are protected with encryption. Keys are held through the operating-system credential facility and access is restricted to authorized administrators. If an original KTP is accepted as physical collateral, the authorized administrator must keep it in secure, access-controlled storage only for the confirmed custody period and return it promptly when the agreed return conditions are met. The application does not store the KTP content, image, or raw OCR output. J2 Outdoor must maintain a handover and return record without copying unnecessary KTP details.

No system is completely risk-free. J2 Outdoor applies reasonable technical and organizational measures, limits access and use to stated purposes, and handles incidents in accordance with legal obligations.

Request drafts that do not become transactions are retained for 30 days after the last activity and then securely deleted. After a verified account-deletion request, the account, credentials, session tokens, and profile data that are no longer required are deleted within 30 days. Transaction, payment, deposit, return, and accounting records that must be retained are kept for 10 years after the end of the relevant financial year. During that period, data is limited to the minimum information needed for legal, tax, accounting, audit, transaction-evidence, security, and dispute-resolution purposes and is not used for marketing.

If a record is needed for an active transaction, suspected fraud, a dispute, an audit, or legal proceedings, deletion of the relevant portion may be deferred until that process is completed. After the retention period or hold ends, Personal Data is securely deleted or anonymized so it can no longer be associated with an individual. Aggregated or permanently de-identified data may be retained. This schedule does not permit retention of a NIK, KTP image, or raw OCR output, which the application does not store.

## 7. Disclosure and WhatsApp

Data may be accessed by assigned J2 Outdoor administrators and technical application or server support providers appointed by J2 Outdoor, only as required to operate the system, under J2 Outdoor's instructions as Personal Data Controller, and subject to appropriate safeguards. Personal Data is not sold.

When you choose to send through WhatsApp, the application opens WhatsApp with a prepared message. Processing by WhatsApp/Meta is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Review the message before sending and do not add unnecessary sensitive identity data.

Google ML Kit Text Recognition processes images and OCR output on the device and does not send them to Google servers. According to Google's disclosure, the SDK collects device information (such as manufacturer, model, operating-system version/build, and ML accelerators), application information (package name and version), performance/utilization metrics, and a per-installation identifier not intended to identify a user or physical device. This diagnostics and usage-analytics data is sent to Google using HTTPS and, according to Google, is not shared with third parties. The SDK may also contact Google for bug fixes, model updates, and accelerator-compatibility information.

When server synchronization is available, the request draft and consent evidence may be transmitted over a protected connection to J2 Outdoor's server so authorized administrators can access it and, if approved, convert it into a rental. If processing or storage occurs across national borders, J2 Outdoor must implement safeguards required by Indonesian law.
